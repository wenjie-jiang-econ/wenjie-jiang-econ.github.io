Welcome! I am Wenjie Jiang, a PhD Candidate in Behavioral Economics at the Erasmus School of Economics, Erasmus University Rotterdam. I received my research master degree in 2025 from the University of Munich.

I am affiliated with the Tinbergen Institute. My research fields are behavioral, cognitive, and experimental economics. I study how people make decisions under complexity and uncertainty, with a particular focus on the cognitive mechanisms underlying economic behavior. My research examines risk and time preferences and moral decision-making through laboratory and online experiments, combined with theoretical modeling and quantitative analysis.

I am jointly supervised by Sophie van der Zee, Georg Granic and Jana Cahlíková.
